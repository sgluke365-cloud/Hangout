# Campfire versions

Each version is saved on GitHub as its own branch (`release-v0.1`, `release-v0.2`, …), so any of them can be put back live. The version number also shows at the bottom of the **?** panel in the game.

| Version | Branch | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v0.6.6 | `release-v0.6.6` | The organ is just a pipe organ now (Flute and Piano are gone), and it can teach you a calming song: Amazing Grace, Brahms' Lullaby or Ode to Joy. The next key to press glows and the one after is outlined; go at your own pace, and a wrong key just gives the right one a little nudge. Listen first has the organ play the song for you while the keys light up. Finish and the pipes glow, a soft chord rings out, and friends in the church see that you played it. Free play is still there. |
| v0.6.5 | `release-v0.6.5` | The pipe organ in the church is playable. Walk up to it and press E (or tap it) to sit at the bench. Play the on-screen keys (tap, hold, or slide across them), or on a computer use A S D F G H J K L ; ' and W E T Y U O P, with Z / X to change octave. Three sounds: Organ, Flute and Piano. Friends in the church hear you through its echo, and anyone in the woods nearby hears it faintly. The pipes glow and little notes drift up while someone plays. One player at the organ at a time. |
| v0.6.4 | `release-v0.6.4` | The inside of the church is now a small ruined cathedral, about three and a half times bigger and laid out like a cross: a long nave of pews between rows of columns with side aisles, great pointed arches overhead, a crossing with a marble compass rose, a side chapel (with the candle stand, a little altar and a stone angel) and a pipe organ in the transepts, and a raised chancel in a many-sided apse with tall windows and a gilded screen behind the altar. Also: banners on the big piers, three chandeliers, a stone font, a confessional, a fallen chandelier and more rubble. Tap-to-walk now finds its way round everything. |
| v0.6.3 | `release-v0.6.3` | The deep woods are thinned out to the same tree density as the rest of the forest. They're still a little darker, and the church is still hidden in the far top-right corner with no path. |
| v0.6.2 | `release-v0.6.2` | The church is properly hidden now. The map is wider: past the old eastern tree line there's a band of thick, dark deep woods, and the church sits in a small clearing in its far top-right corner. The signpost and the trail are gone, so you have to go looking (and the forest devil lives out there). The campsite itself is unchanged. |
| v0.6.1 | `release-v0.6.1` | The church moved to the top-right corner of the woods (signpost by the yellow tent, trail heading east) and got a gothic makeover. Outside: tall traced windows, a deep arched doorway, pinnacles and a crumbling gable. Inside: tall arched windows with moonbeams and drifting dust, a worn checkered marble floor, a faded blue carpet, carved pews, stone columns, golden candelabras, an iron chandelier over the aisle, rubble, and a soft blur at the top and bottom of the screen. The blur switches itself off on devices that can't keep up. |
| v0.6 | `release-v0.6` | The old church: an abandoned church deep in the forest west of the fire (follow the signpost and the overgrown trail). Walk in through the door. Inside it's warm and quiet: pews to sit on, moonlight through the broken roof, stained glass, a sleeping cat, soft music, and no forest scares. Light all nine candles on the candle stand (each touch also flips the ones next to it; Hint and Start over help) and everyone inside gets a talisman. The talisman turns the forest devil away 3 times, shows on your avatar and in the top bar, and is remembered on your device. |
| v0.5.1 | `release-v0.5.1` | Chat messages in the bottom-left fade away completely after 8 seconds so they don't block the campsite. Tap the chat box to bring the recent ones back while you type. |
| v0.5 | `release-v0.5` | Lobbies: the join screen has a Lobby name box. Type any name (friends who type the same name meet at the same fire), or tap the dice for a made-up one. Invite links fill in the lobby for friends, and you see how many people are already there before joining. Your last lobby is remembered on your device. |
| v0.4.1 | `release-v0.4.1` | S'mores instructions: the help panel and README now say you can click and hold **Hold to roast** with the mouse (not just Space), and click **Roast s'mores** / **Serve!**. A short how-to line shows in the s'mores panel until you first roast. The help panel now scrolls on small phones instead of getting cut off. |
| v0.4 | `release-v0.4` | S'mores showdown: anyone at the fire can start a round, friends grab a stick, and everyone roasts at once. Hold to dip the marshmallow into the flames, watch the heat, dodge flare-ups, and serve when it looks golden. Closest to golden wins a crown. Works solo too, with a personal best. S'mores sounds follow the campsite volume slider. |
| v0.3.4 | `release-v0.3.4` | Campsite sound volume: the speaker button in the top bar opens a slider (with mute) for the fire, chimes and forest scares. Friends' voices aren't affected. Your level is remembered on your device; the fire now starts at 50% when you join instead of being off. |
| v0.3.2 | `release-v0.3.2` | Wave, laugh and heart now sit behind one smiley button: tap it to open a small picker, tap one to send it. Gives the chat box more room on phones. |

## Going back to an earlier version

Ask Claude "put v0.2 back live" (or any version). It copies that version onto `main`, and Render redeploys it in 2–3 minutes. Nothing is deleted: the newer versions stay saved on their branches.

# Campfire versions

Each version is saved on GitHub as its own branch (`release-v0.1`, `release-v0.2`, …), so any of them can be put back live. The version number also shows at the bottom of the **?** panel in the game.

| Version | Branch | What changed |

| v0.17 | `release-v0.17` | The stones ask. Each of the seven singing stones (Memory, Laughter, Courage, Longing, Wonder, Truth, Kinship) keeps a question. Stand beside one and **Touch the … stone** (or E): it hums its note and its question appears on a card, edged in the stone's colour, for everyone in the glade ("What is a childhood memory you wish you could relive?"). Touch it again for the next of its three questions. Friends in the glade hear the stone and get a line in their log. Stones you've heard are remembered on your device, and their questions become a new deck, **The Stones**, in the story circle at the fire; its cards show which stone asked. Touching a stone doesn't count towards the song, and the card hides while the door is open. The ? panel's stones paragraph adds one line: each stone keeps a question for whoever touches it. |
| v0.16.2 | `release-v0.16.2` | The windows above the altar speak in riddles now, like clues in a treasure hunt. Each caption is a short verse that hints at its secret (seven sparks woken by unbroken rounds; stones listening where the low sun sets, off the southern path, for the hymn that woke the glass; a devil that fears the gift of nine small flames and never sets foot on holy ground), with no plain how-to and no numbered stones to play. ♪ Hear the line is now ♪ Listen to the glass: it hums the tune and lights the notes over the door. The hymn book's pencil note and the church's hints only point the way: the glass wakes for the first hymn in the organ's book. The ? panel no longer explains how to wake Ember or what the stones want; it keeps how to talk to Ember and the note about the stones listening to your mic. Its church paragraph only hints at the candles now ("wonder what the candles are for… one of them can keep you safe in the woods") instead of saying they give a talisman. |

When Ember has just asked something, the next thing anyone says (within 20 seconds) is taken as the answer. When Ember has just answered you, asking about "you" ("how are you?", "and what about you?") goes to it too. In a group, a question to the circle makes Ember think about joining in sooner, it steps in a little sooner after a pause (2 s, at least 8 s after it last spoke, 15 s between tries), and it always answers when someone seems to be talking to it. A new setting, **Ember joins the conversation**, keeps your words out of it. Ember also answers sooner: when nobody else at the fire has their mic on, what you say goes straight to Ember (no 0.7 s wait for echoes), on phones a shorter pause ends what you said, and the server starts making Ember's voice for the first sentences while the answer is still being written (about one round trip sooner). The speech bubble stays still while Ember talks (it used to bob with Ember's voice); the flames still swell a little as it speaks, more smoothly. ## Going back to an earlier version

Ask Claude "put v0.2 back live" (or any version). It copies that version onto `main`, and Render redeploys it in 2–3 minutes. Nothing is deleted: the newer versions stay saved on their branches.

    People only hear you when they are close by. Sit on a log by the fire to join the story circle: draw a card, share, then pass the talking stick. The dock by the lake is good for quiet fishing. Stay out of the deep forest unless you like surprises.

    The old church: follow the overgrown trail west from the fire (look for the signpost). Inside it's warm and quiet: sit on a pew, read the old book, and light every candle on the candle stand. Solve it and everyone inside gets a talisman that keeps the forest devil away 3 times.

    S'mores showdown: at the fire, click or tap Roast s'mores and friends grab a stick. To roast, press and hold the Hold to roast button with your mouse or finger (or hold Space) to dip the marshmallow into the flames. Let go to pull it out before it gets too hot. Click or tap Serve! when it looks golden. Closest to golden brown wins the crown.

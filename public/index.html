    People only hear you when they are close by. Sit on a log by the fire to join the story circle: draw a card, share, then pass the talking stick. The dock by the lake is good for quiet fishing. Stay out of the deep forest unless you like surprises.

    The old church: somewhere in the deep woods past the eastern tree line, an old church is hidden. There's no path, so you'll have to go looking (the forest devil lives out there too). Inside it's warm and quiet: sit on a pew, read the old book, play the pipe organ, and wonder what the candles are for. The church keeps a few secrets, and one of them can keep you safe in the woods.

    The campfire spirit: the fire has a spirit, fast asleep (watch for the little z's, and listen: it talks in its sleep). How to wake it is a secret, but the old church remembers. Once awake, Ember talks in a bubble over the fire, in its own voice from the flames. Ember follows the conversation at the fire (what's typed, and what's said out loud by anyone with their mic on) and now and then joins in when the friends pause. You don't need its name when you're alone at the fire, when you're answering something Ember just asked, or when you ask it back ("and what about you?"). Otherwise, say or type "Ember, …" ("Ember, tell us a spooky story", "Ember, ask us a question", "Ember, shh", "Goodnight, Ember"), press T to speak once, or tap one of the replies Ember suggests along the bottom. A little flame on the mic button means Ember can hear you; on phones, what you say near the awake fire is turned into text by the server's speech service. Turn off "Ember joins the conversation" below and your words only reach Ember when you speak to it. And Ember isn't the only one out there who loves a good story.

    The singing stones: somewhere in the woods, seven old stones are listening for a song. The old church knows more. The stones only listen while your mic is on and you're inside their ring, and what they hear stays on your device. Each stone also keeps a question for whoever touches it.

    S'mores showdown: at the fire, click or tap Roast s'mores and friends grab a stick. To roast, press and hold the Hold to roast button with your mouse or finger (or hold Space) to dip the marshmallow into the flames. Let go to pull it out before it gets too hot. Click or tap Serve! when it looks golden. Closest to golden brown wins the crown.

    Someone tucked a note between the pages:

    "The devil of the deep wood hates warm light. Light every candle on the stand and this church will give you a charm to carry.

    The candles are old and stubborn. Touch one and the ones beside it flip too. Light them together with friends if you like: whoever is in here when the last one catches gets a charm."

    And in pencil underneath, in a different hand: "The glass above the altar sleeps. It wakes only for the first hymn in the organ's book, played all the way through."
